Trail Safety & Etiquette

Important guidelines for responsible trail riding in Western Australia:

  • Respect land access and private property
  • Follow designated trail routes and signage
  • Check trail conditions before riding
  • Leave no trace and pack out what you pack in

Unsanctioned Trail Areas

Important note about unsanctioned trails:

  • There are many unsanctioned trails out there.
  • The Valley, Mundaring, Byford, Manning Park, Bell's Rapids, Darlington, Glen Forrest, Orange Grove, Kelmscott, Roleystone/Bedfordale, and all of the other unsanctioned trails around Mt Gunjin, Kalamunda, John Forrest, Margaret River, Nannup, Albany, Denmark, and others...
  • We don't share details for you to go riding these trails by yourself, even if they are the best trails WA has to offer. Don't just go ride these because you have heard about them or discovered them online. Find someone to guide you, don't get out of your depth, and know how to respect the wishes of those responsible for trails and the land they're on.

Race-Only Locations

Areas used for organized racing events:

  • There's also Three Chillies Farm and Golden Grove Orchard in Chittering, Evedon Lakeside Retreat in Furguson Valley, and Echidna Park outside Geraldton that have been used for WA Gravity Enduro races. These are not typically open. Wait for races if they happen.
