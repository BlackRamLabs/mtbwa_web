Important Note About Unsanctioned Trails

There are a lot of unsanctioned trails out there.

The Valley, Mundaring, Byford, Manning Park, Bell's Rapids, Darlington, Glen Forrest, Orange Grove, Kelmscott, Roleystone, Bedfordale, Wellington Mills, and all the other unsanctioned trails around Mt Gunjin, Kalamunda, John Forrest, Margaret River, Nannup, Albany, Denmark, and others...

We don't share details for you to go riding these trails by yourself, even if they are the best trails WA has to offer. Don't just go ride these because you have heard about them or discovered them online. Find someone to guide you, don't get out of your depth, and know how to respect the wishes of those responsible for the trails and the land they're on.

Race-Only Locations

There's also Three Chillies Farm and the Golden Grove Orchard in Chittering, Evedon Lakeside Retreat in the Furguson Valley, and Echidna Park outside Geraldton that have been used for WA Gravity Enduro races. These are not typically open. Wait for races if they happen.
