Why MTBWA Certification?

Alternative industry certifications often fail the specific needs of Western Australian riders and instructors. We’ve identified the gaps and built a framework that works for you in WA.

The Industry Gap

  • Lack of local course availability
  • Overlap of learning and assessment
  • Not designed for WA riding area profiles
  • Typically no real-world practice
  • Focus on riding ability over teaching skill

The MTBWA Solution

Our certifications are born from Black Ram MTB real-world clinics, taking the rider-first instructing approach to instructor-first training that gets you ready instead of just checking boxes. We integrate psychology, health/wellness, and international coaching standards with a focus on how to teach, not just how to ride. With our approach, you'll be ready to run clinics the minute you receive your certificate.

What Do You Get?

MTBWA certifications are built for the reality of WA MTB trails and the psychology of how riders actually progress. We’ve scrapped the "box-ticking" exercise of traditional courses in favour of a mentor-led education-in-industry development model.

🙋🏻‍♂️

Real Students

"Roleplaying" with other instructors doesn't let you teach. Shadow a certified instructor to observe, lead drills with real riders to practice, and have students actually learn from you!

🚲

Dedicated Practice

Get low-pressure sessions to "find your voice" before any pass/fail assessment. Your final assessment happens once you are ready—and we allow repeats if desired.

🎓

Accessible Authority

Top athletes have coaches who are pro teachers. We empower you to master skills instruction at your riding level, answering from understanding rather than showing off.

🔬

Applied Science

Understand the physics and psychology of riding. Forget "heavy feet, light hands" clichés; answer the tough questions with logic instead of catchphrases.

🗓️

Your Schedule

Join scheduled clinics and training as your life allows. Mentoring is flexible, and your final assessment is scheduled around you.

✅

Controlled Freedom

Teach on up to Black rated trails where appropriate. We focus on safe technique where your students are actually riding, not arbitrary trail colors.

🧩

Modular Growth

Start with Base and stack your skills. Add Jumps, Drops, or Rough Stuff modules to unlock every technical trail feature as you evolve.

💰

Get To Work

Don't stop once you're certified. Join team sessions with other pros, and get paid to run your own sessions as a casual coach when available.

🤜🤛

A Community

We're local. From racing to trail building, there are always opportunities for crossed paths, shared discussions, and fun on the bikes.

Your Certification Pathway

Not ready for the full dive? Use the Intro certs to dip your toes in.

• Trail Ready: Help novice riders get ready to tackle singletrack, in an open space/on White trails.

• Core: Beginner-rider focused and perfect for entry-level coaching on White/Green trails.

What Certifications Are Available?

MTBWA Instructor Certifications are available for all levels of MTB skills instructor, wherever you are on your journey. Once you have a 'Foundation: Base' Clinic certification you can chose any Growth Clinic certifications to add more clinic options to your repertoire, or add the appropriate Add-on Module certifications to increase the scope of where you can instruct and/or simply increase your proficiency.
