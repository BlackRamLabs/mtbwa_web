Why MTBWA Certification?

Alternative industry certifications often fail the specific needs of Western Australian riders and instructors. We’ve identified the gaps and built a framework that works for you in WA.

The Industry Gap

  • Limited local course availability
  • Overlap of learning and assessment
  • Not designed for WA riding area profiles
  • Typically no real-world practice
  • Focus on riding ability over teaching skill
  • Graduates lack confidence to lead sessions

The MTBWA Solution

Our certifications are born from Black Ram MTB real-world clinics, taking the rider-first instructing approach to instructor-first training that gets you ready instead of just checking boxes. We integrate psychology, health/wellness, and international coaching standards with a focus on how to teach, not just how to ride. With our approach, you'll be ready to run clinics the minute you receive your certificate.

What Do You Get?

MTBWA certifications are built for the reality of WA MTB trails and the psychology of how riders actually progress. We’ve scrapped the "box-ticking" exercise of traditional courses in favour of a mentor-led education-in-industry development model.

MTBWA Instructor Certifications are available for all levels of MTB skills instructor, wherever you are on your journey. Once you have a 2.0 Foundations certification, you can choose any Growth 'Clinic' certification to add more clinic options to your repertoire, or add the appropriate 'Add-on Module' certification to increase the scope of where you can instruct and/or simply increase your proficiency.

Level 1. Introduction

These entry-level certifications are for new instructors who are not confident in their riding/instructing abilities. They focus on teaching core riding skills and building new rider confidence.

1.0 Basics 1.1 Beginners

Level 2. Foundations

The most involved certification, that covers teaching the essential riding skills and techniques that all riders should constantly work to refine.

Assessment Procedure

🎯

Real-world assessment for real-world coaching

You are assessed through a comprehensive combination of questioning by your Instructor Trainer and evaluated on risk management, riding demonstrations, coaching delivery, and question handling while instructing. This ensures you not only understand the subject matter but can effectively apply your knowledge when it matters most.

🏃

Clinic Scope

Assessed during real-world sessions where you'll typically run the group clinic that maps to your certification. Private sessions are also available for personalized assessment.

Setting: Actual group clinics with real students.
🎭

Module Assessment

When demonstrations are required, your Instructor Trainer will assess you as you teach them or other instructor students.

Setting: Controlled practice with Instructor Trainer and/or instructor peers.
