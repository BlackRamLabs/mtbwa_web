Why MTBWA Certification?

Alternative industry certifications often fail the specific needs of Western Australian riders and coaches. We've identified the gaps and built a framework that works for you.

The Industry Gap

Alternative industry certifications often fail the specific needs of Western Australian riders and coaches.

  • ❌ Focus on riding ability over teaching skill
  • ❌ Graduates lack confidence to lead sessions
  • ❌ One-size-fits-all approach doesn't work for WA conditions
  • ❌ Limited practical teaching experience

The MTBWA Solution

Our certifications are born from Black Ram MTB real-world clinics, taking the rider-first coaching approach to coach-first training that gets you ready instead of just checking boxes.

  • ✅ Real-world WA trail experience
  • ✅ Psychology and wellness integration
  • ✅ Focus on teaching methodology
  • ✅ Mentor-led development model

What Do You Get?

MTBWA certifications are built for the reality of WA MTB trails and the psychology of how riders actually progress. We've scrapped the "box-ticking" exercise of traditional courses in favour of a mentor-led education-in-industry development model.

Applied Science

Understand the physics and psychology of riding. Forget clichés; answer the tough questions with logic instead of catchphrases.

📍 Certification Levels

🌟

Level 0. Introduction

Optional starting points

These entry-level certifications are for new coaches who are not confident in their riding/coaching abilities, or for those who are in the process of a Level 1: Foundations certification. They focus on teaching core riding skills and building new rider confidence.

0.1 Basics 0.2 Beginners
✨ Perfect For You If:
  • You want to build confidence in teaching
  • You're only working with beginner riders
  • You prefer starting with core fundamentals
🔨

Level 1: Foundations

The skills for all rider levels

The most involved certification, that covers teaching the essential riding skills and techniques that all riders should constantly work to refine.

1 Foundations
✨ Perfect For You If:
  • You want comprehensive teaching skills
  • You plan to teach above beginner riders
  • You want to improve your own riding
  • You're willing to forget what you think you know
🔨

Knowledge Modules

Add-on understanding

These expand your knowledge and expertise (without adding scope to your instruction limitations).

Women Kids Bike Handling Manoeuvres
✨ Perfect For You If:
  • You want to personalise your instruction
  • You haven't had your instruction reviewed for a while
  • You're always wanting to improve
🔨

Level 2: Growth

Expand your coaching scope

For coaches whoe build on your Level 1: Foundations certification. You can choose any combination of these to expand your coaching scope. Air (jumps and drops) certs are for coaching on rollable technical trail features.

+ Corners + Rough + Speed + Jumps + Drops
✨ Perfect For You If:
  • You're an experienced Level 1: Foundations coach
  • You have questions on detailed riding techniques
  • You want to improve your own riding
  • You want to teach an expanded set of clinic and content
📌

Level 3: Advanced

You're a master of your craft

When you have completed all Level 2: Growth certifications, you are considered a Level 3: Advanced level coach.

🏆
Achievement Unlocked!

Complete all Growth certifications to earn this level

✨ What This Means:
  • You've acquired a broad range of knowledge and techniques
  • You can teach across all MTB skills in many different situations
  • You're an expert skills coach
⚡

Level 4: Elite

Push the limits of riding a bike

Expanded risk management and technical assessment. These add to your coaching scope, allowing you to instruct on more challenging terrain and features.

4.0 Mandatory Air (Jumps & Drops) 4.1 Extreme Terrain (All Trail Ratings)
✨ Perfect For You If:
  • You're already an experienced Level 3: Advanced coach working with advanced riders
  • You happily ride:
    • 4.0 = Steep gap jumps
    • 4.1 = Extreme terrain (double black tech and flow)
  • Risk management is part of your coaching subconscious
  • You're ready for the highest level of coach challenge

Coach Trainer Certifying Coach Trainers

Assessment Procedure

You are assessed through a comprehensive combination of questioning by your Coach Trainer and evaluated on risk management, riding demonstrations, coaching delivery, and question handling while coaching. This ensures you not only understand the subject matter but can effectively apply your knowledge when it matters most.

Clinic Scope

Assessed during real-world sessions where you'll typically run the group clinic that maps to your certification. Private sessions are also available for personalized assessment.

Setting: Actual group clinics with real students.

Module Assessment

When demonstrations are required, your Coach Trainer will assess you as you teach them or other coach students.

Setting: Controlled practice with Coach Trainer and/or coach peers.
